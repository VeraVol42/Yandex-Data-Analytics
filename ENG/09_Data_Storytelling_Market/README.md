## 🍽 Moscow Food Service Market Analysis

This project focuses on exploring the food service market in Moscow, analyzing its structure, and identifying key trends.

#### 🔍 Key Objectives:  
- Determine the number of establishments by type  
- Analyze the distribution of restaurants across streets and districts  
- Evaluate market trends and promising directions  

#### 🛠 Tools Used:  
- **Python**: `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Matplotlib`, `Seaborn`, `Plotly`, `re`, `requests`, `json`  
- **GIS**: `Folium`  
- **Jupyter Notebook**
